Lantern Notes Terms of Use

Lantern Notes is a fictional, free note-taking app used to show that Specter stays quiet when there is little to review.

1. Using the app

You can use Lantern Notes for free. There is no paid plan and no trial.

2. Your notes

Your notes stay on your device. You own everything you write, and you can export or delete it at any time.

3. Changes to these terms

If we change these terms, we will show the new version in the app before it applies.

4. Contact

Questions about these terms can be sent to the fictional address hello@lantern.example.
